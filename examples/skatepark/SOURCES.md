# Demo Sources

The repository intentionally does not include third-party video files.

Before adding local clips under `input/`, record one row per source:

| Local filename | Original URL | Creator | License | Attribution required? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `clip-03.mp4` | paste URL here | creator name | e.g. CC0 / Pexels license | yes/no |

Use only public-domain or appropriately licensed footage. Keep the license terms and attribution requirements with the demo notes, and do not upload footage containing identifiable people unless the license and consent allow redistribution.
